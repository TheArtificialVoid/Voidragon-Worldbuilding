Ventson Trees are large trees found all over [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]], mostly in [[Locations on Planets/Rise Forests\|Rise Forests]]. Named after [[Unique People/lor Ventson\|lor Ventson]], they are harvested for their wood and are often replanted in mass scale.

They are Size 6 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
