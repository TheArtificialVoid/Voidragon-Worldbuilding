Flifish are fish-like organisms that use wings to traverse from ocean to sky. 

They mostly live around the equator of [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Nerou\|Nerou]], breeding in and around [[Locations on Planets/Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands\|Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands]].

They are Size 4 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
