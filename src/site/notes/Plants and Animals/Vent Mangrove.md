Vent Mangroves are a species of floating mangrove that live together in [[Locations on Planets/Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands\|Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands]]. Although they are somewhat commonplace, they are often burnt down to make way for ships to pass through different areas in [[Planet Ocean Locations/Yearnara\|Yearnara]].

Many different subspecies of Vent Mangrove exist, including a smaller subspecies that lives on the [[Planet Ocean Locations/Yearnara\|Yearnara]] ocean floor, usally on the edges of [[Planet Ocean Locations/Aumornian Coral Fields\|Aumornian Coral Fields]] and [[Planet Ocean Locations/Risen Shallows\|Risen Shallows]], forming the sub-biome [[Planet Ocean Locations/Onik's Forest Floor\|Onik's Forest Floor]].

Vent Mangroves are named after [[Unique People/lor Ventson\|lor Ventson]].
