Libiyawood Trees are an endangered species of supermassive tree, that can only be found in [[Dimensional Stuff/The Othergarden\|The Othergarden]], and [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]].

The most famous Libiyawood Tree is [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]], a smaller specimen found on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]].

Libiyawood tree sap and leaves have healing properties, that if mixed in with other chemicals and other ingredients, allow for some of the best medicine in the [[Planetary Systems/Quilifen System\|Quilifen System]].

They are Size 9 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
