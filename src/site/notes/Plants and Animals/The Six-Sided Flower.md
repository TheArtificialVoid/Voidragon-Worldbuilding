Although the original name for the The Six-Sided Flower is lost to time, some knowledge of the plant is still known by very few. The Six-Sided Flower is a almost extinct plant that used to be found throughout the home-worlds of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]]. This plant was a large cultural significance for the Voidragons, but as the home worlds of the Voidragons collapsed, only one specimen has survived.

Aria has a necklace with the jewel on the front in the shape of the flower, with the last seed of the Six-Sided Flower within.

The six-sided flower grows like a large tree, and when the tree is fully grown, the flowers open up, shooting flower petals into the air.

The six-sided flower is a Size 6 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
