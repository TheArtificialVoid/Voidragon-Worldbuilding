Skyhaunts are large, flying creatures that dominate the skys of [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Nerou\|Nerou]]. Although their land-based counterparts have been hunted to extinction on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]], many of their ocean-based counterparts still call other landmasses, like the [[Locations on Planets/Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands\|Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands]] home.

Skyhaunts grab prey from the ocean and bring up to the sky, dropping them against the mangrove islands, killing them instantly. They then pick apart the prey, wasting most of the carcass.

They are Size 5 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Size.
