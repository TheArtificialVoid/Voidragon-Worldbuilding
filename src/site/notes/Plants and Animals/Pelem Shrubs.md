Pelem Shrubs are small shrub-like bushes that grow in the [[Locations on Planets/Rise Forests\|Rise Forests]] on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]]. Harvested for their [[Items and Technology/Pelelms\|Pelelms]], a small chocolate like nut that grows from its flowers. 

They are Size 3 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
