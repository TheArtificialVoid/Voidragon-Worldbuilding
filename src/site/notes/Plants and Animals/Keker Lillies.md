Keker Lillies are large Lilly-like plants that live on the edges of the [[Planet Ocean Locations/Phreatol Ocean\|Phreatol Ocean]], and the [[Planet Ocean Locations/Magnoian Ocean\|Magnoian Ocean]] of the planet [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]].

They grow throughout the coasts of each ocean, covering the beaches with their lava-proof flower petals and littering the sky with their spores. They are the main food of [[Plants and Animals/Stoneskin\|Stoneskin]], and are even used in many culinary dishes throughout the [[Planetary Systems/Quilifen System\|Quilifen System]].

They are Size 6 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
