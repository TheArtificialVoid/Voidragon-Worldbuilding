The Nerouian Light Festival is a celebration of both the change of the new season, [[Events/Alivell Season\|Alivell Season]], the shedding of the leaves from the [[Plants and Animals/Libiyawood Tree\|Libiyawood Tree]] that drift into the city of [[Structures and Cities/Biyawood\|Biyawood]]. 

During the event, throughout the day, preparations are made to the town to celebrate, putting up festive decorations around the town, and placing large woven baskets outside of residences. During the sunset of the day, [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]] begins to glow brighter than any other day of the year, the as [[Species and Civilsations/Nerouian People\|Nerouian People]] have dinner outside, within eye-view of the tree. As soon as the sun completely sets, glowing leaves fall from the tree, drifting into the ocean and the town of [[Structures and Cities/Biyawood\|Biyawood]]. Young Neroians collect as many leaves as they can in the baskets and give them to the townfolk, and are rewarded by their parents with [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]]-shaped [[Items and Technology/Kauri lollies\|Kauri lollies]].

As midnight approaches, the tree loses enough of its leaf matter, and begins to glow dimmly. Once enough leaves have fallen, the tree becomes dim, and many of the leaves drift further inland, or out into [[Planet Ocean Locations/Yearnara\|Yearnara]].
