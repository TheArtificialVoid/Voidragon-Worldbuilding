Eltlin the Warrior is an old warrior from [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]], one of the leaders of the [[Species and Civilsations/The Protectors of the Machine\|The Protectors of the Machine]]. Once an well-known and respected leader, he fled from [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]] after world-wide backlash from being too powerful for his people. He flew to [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Regolanith\|Regolanith]] and now made his life mission to fix the [[Structures and Cities/Ancient Terraformers\|Ancient Terraformers]] that populate the surface, due to his love of the environment, and his interest in fixing the [[Structures and Cities/Ancient Thermal Plants\|Ancient Thermal Plants]] on [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]].

He currently resides in the [[Structures and Cities/Dryaterraformer\|Dryaterraformer]], living a peaceful life, but is always training to be stronger, and to one day take back [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]] by force.
