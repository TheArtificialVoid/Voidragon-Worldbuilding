Jude Gonel was the judge of the [[Structures and Cities/Pyroxena Gold National Courtroom\|Pyroxena Gold National Courtroom]] before its destruction during the [[Events/Aria Voidragon Warcrime Case\|Aria Voidragon Warcrime Case]]. A violent judge, usually sentencing many to death or torture for any sort of crime above the verdict of basic crimes.
