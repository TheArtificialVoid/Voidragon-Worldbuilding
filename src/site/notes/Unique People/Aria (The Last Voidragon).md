Aria is the last of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]]* , and one of the most powerful entities in the known universe. 

Aria is a 5'11 Voidragon, whom is fairly shy. Socially inept, many folks around her think she may have autism. She is the Void [[Magic and Plans/Paradox\|Paradox]], using void based powers during her fights.

Many thousands of years after the extinction of the Voidragon people, Aria fell from space, landing in the middle of the desert on the planet [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]], causing a very large impact crater. She was discovered by [[Unique People/Outsider Koraan Watebring\|Outsider Koraan Watebring]], being left in rags and left with an [[Items and Technology/Ancient Voidragon Necklace\|Ancient Voidragon Necklace]]. 

She was brought to [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]], an old water drilling station in the [[Locations on Planets/Deadman's Barrens\|Deadman's Barrens]]. After Korann looked after Aria for several months, and teaching her [[Language/Quilifen Standard Language\|Quilifen Standard Language]] and how to defend herself from fauna from the desert, they we attacked by automated ancient technology, nicknamed [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]]. After losing [[Unique People/Outsider Koraan Watebring\|Outsider Koraan Watebring]] and the station, she roams the system for both the answer to her arrival, and to understand what attacked her and for what reason.

*With the exception of [[Unique People/The Architect\|The Architect]].*
