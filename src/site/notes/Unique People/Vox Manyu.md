Vox is a Pyxone lawyer situated in [[Structures and Cities/Old Norfern\|Old Norfern]], working on several cases reguarding [[Events/The Glass War\|The Glass War]] and [[Events/Aria Voidragon Warcrime Case\|Aria Voidragon Warcrime Case]].

Originally an engineer for the [[Species and Civilsations/Pyxone National Army\|Pyxone National Army]], he transferred to being a lawyer after being kicked out of the army for a rage-based destruction of a large army settlement. 

Enlisted to help Aria and her friends after they committed a massive warcrime against both nations in [[Events/The Glass War\|The Glass War]], He almost successfully proves his case in the courtroom against one of the head [[Species and Civilsations/Pyxone Infernodes\|Pyxone Infernodes]] after almost proving their case in the [[Structures and Cities/Pyroxena Gold National Courtroom\|Pyroxena Gold National Courtroom]], before the courtroom and most of its inhabitants are destroyed by a bombing attack from [[Species and Civilsations/Thornarites\|Thornarites]] weaponry.
