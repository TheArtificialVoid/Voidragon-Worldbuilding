The Architect is an ancient, almost completely cybernetic descendent of the last of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]]. After an almost complete body modification into cybernetic parts, he lives attached to an ancient voidragon facility deep within [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-3 (Forlorn)\|VDR-3 (Forlorn)]], called [[Structures and Cities/Forlorn Bunker\|Forlorn Bunker]]. 

The Architect sends out reformated [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]] to scout planets, looking for ancient relics of [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]], and to find any remaining members of his species.

Filled with unbridled rage at the death of his species, he watched as the rest of his kind died after [[Events/The Voidragon Collapse\|The Voidragon Collapse]]. Hiding himself in a bunker deep within [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-3 (Forlorn)\|VDR-3 (Forlorn)]], he merged himself with ancient Voidragon technology so he could live forever, and discover what caused the end of his kind.

After destroying [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]] after discovering misuse of Voidragon technology, and then discovering [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]], he made it his mission to kill Aria or bring her back to the bunker.
