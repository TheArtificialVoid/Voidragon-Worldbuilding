Outsider Korann Watebring is an [[Species and Civilsations/Psynovates\|psynovates]] outcast from [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|haloora]]n society, who lives in [[Locations on Planets/Deadman's Barrens\|Deadman's Barrens]] looking after the last settlement in the region, [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]]. He has lived their alone for most of his life, only venturing out of the Barrens to gather supplies from townships on the border.

After finding [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]] in the middle of the desert, being attacked by a swarm of [[Plants and Animals/Rauwing\|Rauwing]], he jumps to action, rescuing her with his strange [[Items and Technology/Ancient halberd\|Ancient halberd]] and guiding her back to [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]]. After several months of teaching her basic [[Language/Quilifen Standard Language\|Quilifen Standard Language]], and basic combat training to protect herself from hostile [[Locations on Planets/Deadman's Barrens\|Deadman's Barrens]] fauna, he died to a group of [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]] who destroyed the station, but allowing Aria to escape out of the desert.

Koraan's grandparents caused a major crisis 100 years ago, which caused their entire bloodline to be shamed by many people from [[Locations on Planets/Scoranta\|Scoranta]]. After Koraan's passing, the exclusion of their bloodline from society will be exempt.
