Kauri lollies are Gelatin-like lollies, that have small chocolate-like [[Items and Technology/Pelelms\|Pelelms]] inside. Famous for their treat-like status, they are given out plenty during the [[Events/Nerouian Light Festival\|Nerouian Light Festival]] to younger [[Species and Civilsations/Nerouian People\|Nerouian People]]. It is one of the largest businesses on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]], and is sold throughout the system.

During [[Events/Nerouian Light Festival\|Nerouian Light Festival]], they are made in the shape of [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]], the [[Plants and Animals/Libiyawood Tree\|Libiyawood Tree]] found on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]].
