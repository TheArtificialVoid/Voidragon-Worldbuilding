The Ancient Halberd is an old weapon that was used by [[Unique People/Outsider Koraan Watebring\|Outsider Koraan Watebring]], to defend [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]] from outside fauna and [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]].

Koraan supposedly found the blade in the same location, as [[Locations on Planets/Aria's Planetfall Crater\|Aria's Planetfall Crater]], but 20 years earlier. The blade was in pristine condition, even though it had fallen from space, and after use after the last 20 years, still remains largely pristine. Koraan cleans the blade consistently, keeping it as his pride and joy.

During the [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]]' attack on [[Structures and Cities/Watebring station\|Watebring station]], Koraan lost his life, and the Ancient Halberd was taken in by [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]].
