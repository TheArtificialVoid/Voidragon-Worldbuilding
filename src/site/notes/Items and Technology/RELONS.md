RELONS are strange, ancient technological marvels that have only been spotted a few times over the course of the last 400 years. Made of ancient alloys, and amazingly quick, they are the most efficient automated force in the entire galaxy.

Due to their make, and the fact that very few have been disassembled, it is theorized that they originate from [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]]' technology, due to similar history and possible links to the [[Voidragon Stuff/Lost Voidragon Homeworlds\|Lost Voidragon Homeworlds]] From the few that have been disassembled, technology across the [[Planetary Systems/Quilifen System\|Quilifen System]] has been boosted dramatically, and have allowed for trans-planetary-system travel.
