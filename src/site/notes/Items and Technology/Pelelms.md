Pelelms are small chocolate like beads that are grown from [[Plants and Animals/Pelem Shrubs\|Pelem Shrubs]], found in [[Locations on Planets/Rise Forests\|Rise Forests]] on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]]. They are very yummy and have a slightly pineapple-like taste.
