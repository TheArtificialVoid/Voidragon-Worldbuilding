The Ancient Voidragon Necklace is an ancient alloy necklace with a gem in the shape of the head of [[Plants and Animals/The Six-Sided Flower\|The Six-Sided Flower]]. Inside the gem, is an unknown seed, theorized to be the last seed of the real [[Plants and Animals/The Six-Sided Flower\|The Six-Sided Flower]], which is the only known flora from the [[Voidragon Stuff/Lost Voidragon Homeworlds\|Lost Voidragon Homeworlds]].

[[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]] wears this necklace at all times, and due to its unknown properties, somehow stopped her from death while impacting [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]] for the first time.
