Biyawood is a large Neurionian settlement based on the north side of the [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]]. Famous for its location near [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]], tourists flock here throughout the [[Events/Alivell Season\|Alivell Season]] and late into the [[Events/Florun Season\|Florun Season]].

Leaves and sticks from [[Plants and Animals/Kaurino\|Kaurino]] rain down on the city periodically, usually during the [[Events/Alivell Season\|Alivell Season]]. Due to the healing properties from the leaves fallen from the tree, many tourists gather in the city during the season, commencing the [[Events/Nerouian Light Festival\|Nerouian Light Festival]].

The City is named after the wood from the [[Plants and Animals/Libiyawood Tree\|Libiyawood Tree]], which was also used to make many of the houses in the suburbs of the city. Due to the location of the city, many of the leaves from the tree fall into the shore and streets of the city, allowing for easy collection of the leaf-matter.
