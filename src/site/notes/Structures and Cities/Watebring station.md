Watebring station was a water collection station found in the [[Locations on Planets/Deadman's Barrens\|Deadman's Barrens]]. Once home to [[Unique People/Outsider Koraan Watebring\|Outsider Koraan Watebring]], his family was forced to move there due to a major crisis 100 years ago in the [[Locations on Planets/Scoranta\|Scoranta]] region. 

Watebring station was partially used to train [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]], and gave her a place to live for several months.

After Watebring station was destroyed by [[Items and Technology/RELONS\|RELONS]], and after the disappearance of [[Unique People/Outsider Koraan Watebring\|Outsider Koraan Watebring]], the station was scrapped for parts by the [[Species and Civilsations/Segekel Foundation\|Segekel Foundation]].
