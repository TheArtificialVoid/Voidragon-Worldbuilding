Rise forests are forests of [[Plants and Animals/Ventson Trees\|Ventson Trees]], named after [[Unique People/lor Ventson\|lor Ventson]].

They are large-scale, and are harvested consistently for their resources. [[Plants and Animals/Pelem Shrubs\|Pelem Shrubs]] grow consistently throughout the Ventson Forests.
