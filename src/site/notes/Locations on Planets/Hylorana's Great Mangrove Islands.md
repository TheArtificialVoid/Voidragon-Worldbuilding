Hylorana's Great Mangrove Islands (or Floating Forests) are mangrove-like forests that float ontop of the surface of [[Planet Ocean Locations/Yearnara\|Yearnara]], [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Nerou\|Nerou]]'s world-covering ocean. Although large-scale, these islands float atop and move with the wind/currents, and allow for shelter for any [[Plants and Animals/Flifish\|Flifish]] and [[Plants and Animals/Skyhaunts\|Skyhaunts]].

The mangrove islands are made up of several different species of [[Plants and Animals/Vent Mangrove\|Vent Mangrove]]. Their roots intertwine within one another and form the base of the island, allowing for dirt and plant matter to pile up, at the center of these great islands.
