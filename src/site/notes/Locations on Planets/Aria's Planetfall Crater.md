Aria's Halooran Planetfall Crater is a large crater in the middle of [[Locations on Planets/Deadman's Barrens\|Deadman's Barrens]] in [[Locations on Planets/Scoranta\|Scoranta]] on [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]]. Both the [[Items and Technology/Ancient halberd\|Ancient halberd]] and [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]].

A strange pool of black liquid has formed in the centre.
