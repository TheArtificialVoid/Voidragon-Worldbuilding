Pyroxena is the southern continent found at the south pole of the planet [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]]. 

Home to the [[Species and Civilsations/Pyxones\|Pyxones]], the southern continent is also known as "The Land of Red Dusk". 

Pyroxena is the home to [[Unique People/Vox Manyu\|Vox Manyu]].
