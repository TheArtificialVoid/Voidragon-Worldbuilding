This dimension is most similar to ours, and is where the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]] Civilization arose. 

Although there are millions of different galaxies through The Starzone, The [[Universe Locations/Grandway Galaxy\|Grandway Galaxy]], once the home of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]], is where most of the stories within [[Dimensional Stuff/The Universe of the Voidragons\|The Universe of the Voidragons]] take place. Other galaxies are documented, but due to limitations within space travel from most other civilizations, they are almost impossible to get to. 

The space between star clusters within this dimension is called [[Universe Locations/Deep Space\|Deep Space]], and is an empty area of space that separates the galaxies from one another. There are many different artifacts from populated spaces from star clusters within Deep Space, such as [[The Othergarden/The Forgotten Gateway\|The Forgotten Gateway]], or the [[Structures and Cities/The Remaining VIGT\|The Remaining VIGT]].
