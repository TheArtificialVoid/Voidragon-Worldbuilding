Lumino is the parent star to the plantary system, The [[Planetary Systems/Salyut System\|Salyut System]]. The remnants of a Dyson Swarm can be found orbiting the star.

The planets [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-1 (Utoma'tar)\|VDR-1 (Utoma'tar)]], [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-2 (Refineda)\|VDR-2 (Refineda)]], and [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-3 (Forlorn)\|VDR-3 (Forlorn)]] can be found orbiting the planet, with [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Aeromon (VDHW)\|Aeromon (VDHW)]], an extinct planet, found in its orbit.
