The Eyeyona Super-Cluster is an eye-shaped galaxy supercluster that is made up of the [[Universe Locations/Grandway Galaxy\|Grandway Galaxy]] and several other galaxies. Although they have names, it is still disputed what they should be called, due to their discoveries being found at different times, by different civilizations. 

So far, they have been standardized as Eyeyona-1 though to Eyeyona-9.
