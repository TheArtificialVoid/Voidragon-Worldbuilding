Aeromon was one of the homeworlds of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]] before [[Events/The Voidragon Collapse\|The Voidragon Collapse]]. It was the smallest of the extinct homeworlds, 

The planet is currently destroyed, but evidence of the planet being in its orbit show in the other orbits of [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-2 (Refineda)\|VDR-2 (Refineda)]], [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-1 (Utoma'tar)\|VDR-1 (Utoma'tar)]], and [[Moons, Planets and Stars/VDR-3 (Forlorn)\|VDR-3 (Forlorn)]]. Some ancient technology is still found orbiting where the planet once was. 

Not much is known about this planet, outside of being the home of The [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]], [[Unique People/Aria (The Last Voidragon)\|Aria (The Last Voidragon)]] and the species of [[Plants and Animals/The Six-Sided Flower\|The Six-Sided Flower]].
