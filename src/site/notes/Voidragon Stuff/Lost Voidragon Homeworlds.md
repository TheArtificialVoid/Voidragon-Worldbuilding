The Lost Homeworlds are the previous home of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]], before their extinction during [[Events/The Voidragon Collapse\|The Voidragon Collapse]]. 

The lost homeworlds were made up of many different habitable planets throughout the galaxy, including;
- [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Aeromon (VDHW)\|Aeromon (VDHW)]]
- [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Galaxienrand - (Extinct Galaxy's Edge Voidragon Homeworld)\|Galaxienrand - (Extinct Galaxy's Edge Voidragon Homeworld)]]
- [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Nullarquall - (Galaxy Center's Voidragon Homeworld)\|Nullarquall - (Galaxy Center's Voidragon Homeworld)]]
- [[Moons, Planets and Stars/The First Voidragon Homeworld (Fexix)\|The First Voidragon Homeworld (Fexix)]]

Due to the extinction of the Voidragon species, and the destruction of their homeworld planets, history of the planets and their civilisation, life and much more is missing.
