Oortermina is a terminal being disease that spreads through both organic [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]] cells and through the then recently developed [[Voidragon Stuff/Nerolor Connecting System\|Nerolor Connecting System]]. Due to the circumstances at the time of the diseases' peak, almost all members of the [[Voidragon Stuff/Voidragons\|Voidragons]] were cast to extinction.
