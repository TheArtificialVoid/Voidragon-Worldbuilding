Gardensomnia is a insomnia-based condition in which people are completely unable to sleep within [[Dimensional Stuff/The Othergarden\|The Othergarden]], no matter how hard they try. This condition has not been completely studied, although a few tests have been done by [[The Othergarden/Gardeneers\|Gardeneers]] have discovered that

- People sleeping being brought in from [[Dimensional Stuff/The Starzone\|The Starzone]] are imminently woken up as soon as they are brought into [[Dimensional Stuff/The Othergarden\|The Othergarden]] through one of the portals.
- People who are knocked unconscious from within the dimension either instantly wake up, or have a very small chance to instantly die.
- Unconscious people brought into the dimension have a 50/50 chance to either wake up after passing through the portal, or are turned into plantmatter.
- If turned to plantmatter, if deemed worthwhile, [[Unique People/The Gardener\|The Gardener]] may revive them with the cost of their memories.
