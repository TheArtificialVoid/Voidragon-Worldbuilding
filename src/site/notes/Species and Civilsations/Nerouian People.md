The Nerouian People are mostly made up of the groups [[Species and Civilsations/The Unait\|The Unait]] and [[Species and Civilsations/The Quo\|The Quo]], whom are refugees from the planets [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]] and [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Oriana\|Oriana]]. 

Most of the population of Nerouian People live on [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]], fishing and farming while very few run the towns and government.
