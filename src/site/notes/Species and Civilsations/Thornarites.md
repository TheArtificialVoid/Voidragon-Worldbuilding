'Thor-nare-rites' 

Thornarites are the dominating species for the northern hemisphere on [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]].

They have smaller, naturally armored build that is covered in spikes and thorns. Originally living underground on [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]], they arose to the surface for resources and to move from their home planet, into the stars.

After being in a constant war with the [[Species and Civilsations/Pyxones\|Pyxones]] for thousands of years, due to biological, cultural, and religious differences, the multi-generational war has been put on hold due to lack of resources on both sides.

The war is referred to as [[Events/The Glass War\|The Glass War]] due to how fragile previous peace treaties were.

Thornarites are Size 4 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] Scale.
