Native Untundrians are split into two major factions, [[Species and Civilsations/The Protectors of the Machine\|The Protectors of the Machine]] and [[Species and Civilsations/Untundrian Tribes\|Untundrian Tribes]]. Due to evolving on the much colder climate compared to every other planet in the [[Planetary Systems/Quilifen System\|Quilifen System]], Untundrians are very fluffy, almost yeti like.
