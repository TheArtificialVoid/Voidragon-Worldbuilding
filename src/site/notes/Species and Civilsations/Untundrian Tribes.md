Untundrian Tribes are tribes found although out [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]], made up of native [[Species and Civilsations/Untundrians\|Untundrians]].

There are small Untundrian Tribes that have claimed two of the 42 Thermal Plants, but most reside within the outside of the plants, in [[Locations on Planets/The Winterlands\|The Winterlands]] of the planet.
