'Py-zones'

Pyxones are the dominating species on the southern hemisphere of [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]]. 

After being in a constant war with the [[Species and Civilsations/Thornarites\|Thornarites]] for thousands of years, due to biological, cultural, and religious differences, the multi-generational war has been put on hold due to lack of resources on both sides. The war is referred to as [[Events/The Glass War\|The Glass War]].

Pyxones are described as orange orbs with spikes and feathers, that have a zig-zag opening for a mouth that opens around 50% of their head. They have dark, single coloured eyes, and dark mouths. Although just orbs, they have a humanoid form through hovering over clothing and puppeteering it, allowing for use of hands and legs.

Pyxones when wanting to move quickly, burst wings out of the side of their heads, and fly up into the sky, leaving their clothes on the ground. They can either return to the clothing on the ground to repuppet it, or can find more clothing elsewhere. In their culture, It is considered rude to not be wearing any clothing around other Pyxones.

Pyxones clothing is stylised as 1950's clothing, with suits and dresses making up the majority of the public's fashion.

The leader of [[Locations on Planets/Pyroxena\|Pyroxena]] is [[Unique People/RULER STRADANUS\|RULER STRADANUS]].

Pyxones are Size 4-6 on the [[Information Systems/Y.E.S.C.I.\|Y.E.S.C.I.]] scale.
