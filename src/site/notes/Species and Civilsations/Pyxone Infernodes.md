Pyxone Infernodes are a collection of pyxone individuals who have given up their concept of individuality to form (with many other pyxone individuals) a larger, smarter individual called a Infernode. 

Many smaller infernodes become staples in their community, doing important thought based jobs such as; Judges, mathematicians, physicists, astronomers and (usually) Lawyers and Monarchs. 

Some examples of well known Infernodes include;
