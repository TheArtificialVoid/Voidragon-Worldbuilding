Macrosystem sections are section of [[Dimensional Stuff/The Othergarden\|The Othergarden]] that are the combination of all of the lifen sections within a planetary system from [[Dimensional Stuff/The Starzone\|The Starzone]]. A single Macrosystem section can be broken down into different [[The Othergarden/Lifen section\|Lifen section]]s depending on the planetary system.

For example, the [[The Othergarden/Haloora section\|Haloora section]] hosts all of the plant and fungi-like life within its lifen section, and when paired with the rest of the lifen sections from that area, are then labeled as a [[The Othergarden/Macrosystem section\|Macrosystem section]].
