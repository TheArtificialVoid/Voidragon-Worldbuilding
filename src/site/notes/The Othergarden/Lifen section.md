Lifen sections are section of [[Dimensional Stuff/The Othergarden\|The Othergarden]] that mirrors the environments of the planet or moon from [[Dimensional Stuff/The Starzone\|The Starzone]]. Multiple Lifen sections together make a [[The Othergarden/Macrosystem section\|Macrosystem section]], which is usually based off of the planetary system within [[Dimensional Stuff/The Starzone\|The Starzone]].

For example, the [[The Othergarden/Haloora section\|Haloora section]] hosts all of the plant and fungi-like life within its lifen section, and when paired with the rest of the lifen sections from that area, are then labeled as a [[The Othergarden/Macrosystem section\|Macrosystem section]].
