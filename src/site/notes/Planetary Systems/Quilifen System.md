The Quilifen system is one of the most unique systems within the [[Universe Locations/Grandway Galaxy\|Grandway Galaxy]], with each planet hosting at least one form of intelligent life.

This system hosts 5 separate planets, with conditions that range from scorching hot, to icy cold.

The Star within the system are

[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Ingi\|Ingi]] (The parent star of the system, home to [[Unique People/The Maw\|The Maw]].)

The planets within the system are

[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Mantle\|Mantle]] 
(A blistering planet home to the [[Species and Civilsations/Thornarites\|Thornarites]] and [[Species and Civilsations/Pyxones\|Pyxones]], locked forever in [[Events/The Glass War\|The Glass War]].)
[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]] 
(A planet with many different tropical lands, also home to the [[Species and Civilsations/Psynovates\|Psynovates]].)
[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Oriana\|Oriana]] 
(The most technologically advanced planet, and home to the [[Species and Civilsations/Startails\|Startails]].)
[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Nerou\|Nerou]] 
(An ocean based planet that hosts a large island called [[Locations on Planets/The Rise\|The Rise]]), home to a variety of intelligent species.)
[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Untundria\|Untundria]] 
(A frozen wasteland, dominated by snowy landscapes and dotted with [[Structures and Cities/Ancient Thermal Plants\|Ancient Thermal Plants]] which keep the planet livable.)

The Moons within the system are

[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Rani\|Rani]] 
(A moon orbiting the planet [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Oriana\|Oriana]], used for nuclear tests and experiments.)
[[Moons, Planets and Stars/Regolanith\|Regolanith]] 
(A garden-like moon orbiting [[Moons, Planets and Stars/Haloora\|Haloora]].)
